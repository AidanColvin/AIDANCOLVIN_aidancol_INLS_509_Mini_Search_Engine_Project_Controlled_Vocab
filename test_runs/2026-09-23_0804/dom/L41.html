  4. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Zoloft, sertraline, Motrin, Advil

    2 drugs: risk of serious bleeding, especially stomach and gut bleeding

    Why C: Class rule: risk of serious bleeding, especially stomach and gut bleeding. Clinical rulebook row 9, 12, 13.

    Why it happens

    Anticoagulants slow clotting, aspirin and other antiplatelets stop platelets from sticking, NSAIDs block platelets and injure the stomach lining, and SSRIs and SNRIs drain the serotonin platelets need. Each added drug raises the bleeding risk.

    What to do

    Drop any drug that is not essential (WOEST and AUGUSTUS support dropping aspirin from triple therapy), add a proton pump inhibitor for stomach protection, and watch for black stools, vomiting blood, or anemia.

    FDA labels: Zoloft · sertraline · Motrin · Advil · Dechanont et al., Pharmacoepidemiol Drug Saf 2014: drug interactions in hospital admissions · Dewilde et al., Lancet 2013 (WOEST): dropping aspirin from triple therapy · Lopes et al., NEJM 2019 (AUGUSTUS): antithrombotic therapy after ACS or PCI in atrial fibrillation
