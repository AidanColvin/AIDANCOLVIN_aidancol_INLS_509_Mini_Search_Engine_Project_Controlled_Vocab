    Zyvox, Effexor XR

    Zyvox with Effexor XR: can cause fatal serotonin syndrome

    Why E: The Contraindications section of the Effexor XR label addresses this combination: can cause fatal serotonin syndrome. Clinical rulebook row 7.

    4 CONTRAINDICATIONS Venlafaxine Extended-Release Tablets are contraindicated in patients: with known hypersensitivity to venlafaxine besylate, venlafaxine hydrochloride, desvenlafaxine succinate or to any excipients in Venlafaxine Extended-Release Tablets [see Adverse Reactions ( 6.2 )] . taking, or within 14 days of stopping, MAOIs (including the MAOIs linezolid and intravenous methylene blue) because of increased risk of serotonin syndrome [see Dosage and Administration ( 2.9 ), Warnings and Precautions ( 5.2 ), Drug Interactions ( 7.1 )] .

    From the Effexor XR label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    An MAO inhibitor stops the breakdown of serotonin; these drugs release it or block its reuptake. Together serotonin can rise to toxic levels: high fever, rigidity, seizures, death.

    What to do

    Do not use together. Allow the washout the labels require (usually 14 days after stopping an MAO inhibitor, 5 weeks after fluoxetine).

    FDA labels: Zyvox · Effexor XR · Dunkley et al., QJM 2003: the Hunter serotonin toxicity criteria · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  2. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Duragesic, Robaxin

    2 CNS depressants: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose

    Why D: The Boxed Warning section of the Duragesic label addresses this combination: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose. Clinical rulebook row 1, 2.

    Risks From Concomitant Use With Benzodiazepines Or Other CNS Depressants Concomitant use of opioids with benzodiazepines or other central nervous system (CNS) depressants, including alcohol, may result in profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death.

    From the Duragesic label, Boxed Warning section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug depresses the brain on its own; together the effects add up. Opioids with benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, or other sedatives can stop breathing: both opioid and benzodiazepine labels carry a boxed warning on this combination.

    What to do

    Use the lowest doses for the shortest time, avoid adding more sedatives, and watch for sleepiness and slow or shallow breathing. With an opioid, co-prescribe naloxone.

    FDA labels: Duragesic · Robaxin · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

    5 serotonergic drugs: risk of serotonin syndrome

    4 CONTRAINDICATIONS Venlafaxine Extended-Release Tablets are contraindicated in patients: with known hypersensitivity to venlafaxine besylate, venlafaxine hydrochloride, desvenlafaxine succinate or to any excipients in Venlafaxine Extended-Release Tablets [see Adverse Reactions ( 6.2 )] . taking, or within 14 days of stopping, MAOIs (including the MAOIs linezolid and intravenous methylene blue) because of increased risk of serotonin syndrome [see Dosage and Administration ( 2.9 ), Warnings and Precautions ( 5.2 ), Drug Interactions ( 7.1 )] .

    From the Effexor XR label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug raises serotonin activity. Together they can cause serotonin syndrome: agitation, tremor, clonus and overactive reflexes, sweating, fever, fast heart rate.

    What to do

    Avoid stacking serotonergic drugs where possible; if needed, use low doses and watch for clonus, tremor, fever, and agitation, especially in the first days after a start or dose increase.

    • Zyvox with 2 serotonergic drugs: risk of serotonin syndrome. An MAO inhibitor stops the breakdown of serotonin, and these drugs add serotonin activity. The labels advise against the combination.

    FDA labels: Zyvox · Effexor XR · Buspar · Reglan · Duragesic · Dunkley et al., QJM 2003: the Hunter serotonin toxicity criteria
