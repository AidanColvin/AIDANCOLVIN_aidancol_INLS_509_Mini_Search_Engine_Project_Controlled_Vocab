Why it happens

Each drug depresses the brain on its own; together the effects add up. Opioids with benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, or other sedatives can stop breathing: both opioid and benzodiazepine labels carry a boxed warning on this combination.

What to do

Use the lowest doses for the shortest time, avoid adding more sedatives, and watch for sleepiness and slow or shallow breathing. With an opioid, co-prescribe naloxone.

OxyContin with Klonopin: boxed warning: profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, death. Opioids and benzodiazepines both suppress the brainstem's drive to breathe. Both drug classes carry an FDA boxed warning against this combination.
OxyContin with Neurontin: risk of respiratory depression. Gabapentin and pregabalin add to opioid respiratory depression; the FDA added this warning to both in 2019. Both are cleared by the kidney, so kidney injury raises exposure.
