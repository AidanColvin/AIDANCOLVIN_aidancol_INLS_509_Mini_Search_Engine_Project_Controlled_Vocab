  1. EAvoid combination — Do not use these drugs together.

    Zocor, Biaxin

    Zocor with Biaxin: statin levels rise many-fold: risk of rhabdomyolysis

    Why E: The Contraindications section of the Zocor label addresses this combination: statin levels rise many-fold: risk of rhabdomyolysis. Clinical rulebook row 27.

    4 CONTRAINDICATIONS Simvastatin is contraindicated in the following conditions: • Concomitant use of strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (select azole anti-fungals, macrolide antibiotics, anti-viral medications, and nefazodone) [see DRUG INTERACTIONS ( 7.1 )]. • Concomitant use of cyclosporine, danazol or gemfibrozil [see DRUG INTERACTIONS ( 7.1 )]. • Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis [see WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS ( 5.3 )] • Hypersensitivity to simvastatin or any excipients in simvastatin tablets USP.

    From the Zocor label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Simvastatin and lovastatin are cleared almost entirely by CYP3A4. Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (and erythromycin, cyclosporine, gemfibrozil, danazol) raise their levels roughly 10-fold, causing muscle breakdown and kidney failure. The simvastatin label contraindicates these combinations.

    What to do

    Do not use together. Hold the statin during the antibiotic or antifungal course, or switch to pravastatin or rosuvastatin.

    FDA labels: Zocor · Biaxin · FDA: Drug Development and Drug Interactions, Table of Substrates, Inhibitors and Inducers · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  2. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Aldactone, Vasotec, Klor-Con

    3 drugs: risk of high potassium (hyperkalemia)

    Why D: The Contraindications section of the Aldactone label addresses this combination: risk of high potassium (hyperkalemia). Clinical rulebook row 15.

    4 CONTRAINDICATIONS Spironolactone tablets are contraindicated in the patients with: Hyperkalemia Addison's disease Concomitant use of eplerenone Spironolactone tablets are contraindicated in patients with ( 4 ): Hyperkalemia Addison's disease Concomitant use of eplerenone

    From the Aldactone label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    ACE inhibitors and ARBs lower aldosterone, spironolactone and eplerenone block it, trimethoprim blocks the kidney's potassium channel (like amiloride), and supplements add potassium. Together potassium can rise to levels that stop the heart.

    What to do

    Check potassium and kidney function within 3 days and again at 1 week; stop the potassium supplement or change the antibiotic when possible.

    FDA labels: Aldactone · Vasotec · Klor-Con · Juurlink et al., NEJM 2004: hyperkalemia after spironolactone use rose after RALES · Antoniou et al., CMAJ 2015: trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole with spironolactone and sudden death
