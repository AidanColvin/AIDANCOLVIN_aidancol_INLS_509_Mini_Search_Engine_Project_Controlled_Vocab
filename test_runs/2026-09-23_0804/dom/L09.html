  1. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Prozac, Paxil, Nolvadex

    Nolvadex with 2 drugs that block CYP2D6: tamoxifen may not work

    Why D: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Paxil label addresses this combination: tamoxifen may not work. Clinical rulebook row 35.

    5.11 Reduction of Efficacy of Tamoxifen Some studies have shown that the efficacy of tamoxifen, as measured by the risk of breast cancer relapse/mortality, may be reduced with concomitant use of paroxetine as a result of paroxetine's irreversible inhibition of CYP2D6 and lower blood levels of tamoxifen [see Drug Interactions ( 7 )].

    From the Paxil label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Tamoxifen works through its active metabolite endoxifen, made by CYP2D6. Strong CYP2D6 inhibitors cut endoxifen levels, which has been associated with more breast cancer recurrence (efficacy loss).

    What to do

    Avoid; for depression choose an antidepressant with little CYP2D6 effect (venlafaxine, escitalopram, citalopram).

    FDA labels: Prozac · Paxil · Nolvadex · FDA: Drug Development and Drug Interactions, Table of Substrates, Inhibitors and Inducers

  2. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Prozac, Paxil

    2 serotonin reuptake inhibitors: duplicate therapy, no added benefit, more serotonin syndrome and bleeding risk

    Why D: Class rule: duplicate therapy, no added benefit, more serotonin syndrome and bleeding risk.

    Why it happens

    These are all serotonin reuptake inhibitors: taking more than one is therapeutic duplication.

    What to do

    Keep one antidepressant; cross-taper if switching (fluoxetine's long half-life needs a washout).

    FDA labels: Prozac · Paxil

  3. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Prozac, Paxil

    2 serotonergic drugs: risk of serotonin syndrome

    Why C: The Contraindications section of the Prozac label addresses this combination: risk of serotonin syndrome. Clinical rulebook row 7.

    In addition, do not start fluoxetine tablets in a patient who is being treated with linezolid or intravenous methylene blue (4.1, 7.1) Pimozide (4.2, 5.11, 7.6, 7.7) Thioridazine : Do not use concomitantly with or within 5 weeks of discontinuing fluoxetine tablets (4.2, 5.11, 7.6, 7.7) Known hypersensitivity to fluoxetine products (4.2, 5.3) 4.1 Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs) The use of MAOIs intended to treat psychiatric disorders with fluoxetine tablets or within 5 weeks of stopping treatment with fluoxetine tablets is contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome.

    From the Prozac label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug raises serotonin activity. Together they can cause serotonin syndrome: agitation, tremor, clonus and overactive reflexes, sweating, fever, fast heart rate.

    What to do

    Avoid stacking serotonergic drugs where possible; if needed, use low doses and watch for clonus, tremor, fever, and agitation, especially in the first days after a start or dose increase.

    FDA labels: Prozac · Paxil · Dunkley et al., QJM 2003: the Hunter serotonin toxicity criteria

  4. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Prozac, Paxil, Naprosyn

    3 drugs: risk of serious bleeding, especially stomach and gut bleeding

    Why C: Class rule: risk of serious bleeding, especially stomach and gut bleeding. Clinical rulebook row 9, 12, 13.

    Why it happens

    Anticoagulants slow clotting, aspirin and other antiplatelets stop platelets from sticking, NSAIDs block platelets and injure the stomach lining, and SSRIs and SNRIs drain the serotonin platelets need. Each added drug raises the bleeding risk.

    What to do

    Drop any drug that is not essential (WOEST and AUGUSTUS support dropping aspirin from triple therapy), add a proton pump inhibitor for stomach protection, and watch for black stools, vomiting blood, or anemia.

    FDA labels: Prozac · Paxil · Naprosyn · Dechanont et al., Pharmacoepidemiol Drug Saf 2014: drug interactions in hospital admissions · Dewilde et al., Lancet 2013 (WOEST): dropping aspirin from triple therapy · Lopes et al., NEJM 2019 (AUGUSTUS): antithrombotic therapy after ACS or PCI in atrial fibrillation

  5. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Prozac, Paxil, Toprol-XL

    Toprol-XL with 2 drugs that block CYP2D6: metoprolol levels rise: slower heart rate, lower blood pressure

    Why C: The Drug Interactions section of the Toprol-XL label addresses this combination: metoprolol levels rise: slower heart rate, lower blood pressure.

    ( 7.1 ) CYP2D6 Inhibitors are likely to increase metoprolol concentration.

    From the Toprol-XL label, Drug Interactions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Metoprolol is cleared by CYP2D6; inhibitors raise its level 2- to 5-fold.

    What to do

    Check heart rate and blood pressure; lower the metoprolol dose or switch to a beta-blocker not cleared by CYP2D6 (atenolol, bisoprolol).

    FDA labels: Prozac · Paxil · Toprol-XL · FDA: Drug Development and Drug Interactions, Table of Substrates, Inhibitors and Inducers

  6. AMinor — Minor: usually no action needed.

    Prozac, Paxil, Nexium

    QT Prolongation Risk shared by 3 drugs

    Why A: These labels each mention this risk, but no class rule applies to this combination.

    Such conditions include concomitant use of drugs that prolong the QT interval; hypokalemia or hypomagnesemia; recent myocardial infarction, uncompensated heart failure, bradyarrhythmias, and other significant arrhythmias; and conditions that predispose to increased fluoxetine exposure (overdose, hepatic impairment, use of CYP2D6 inhibitors, CYP2D6 poor metabolizer status, or use of other highly protein-bound drugs).

    From the Prozac label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    What this means: QT prolongation

    Each of these drugs can delay the heart's electrical recovery after a beat, seen as a longer QT interval on an ECG. Together the delay can add up and trigger torsades de pointes, a dangerous heart rhythm.

    Source: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, NIH
