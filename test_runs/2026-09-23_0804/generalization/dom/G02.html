    Zanaflex, Cipro

    Zanaflex with Cipro: tizanidine levels rise 10- to 33-fold: severe low blood pressure and sedation

    Why E: The Drug Interactions section of the Zanaflex label addresses this combination: tizanidine levels rise 10- to 33-fold: severe low blood pressure and sedation. Clinical rulebook row 4.

    Known hypersensitivity to ciprofloxacin injection or other quinolones ( 4.1 , 5.7 ) Concomitant administration with tizanidine ( 4.2 ) 4.1 Hypersensitivity Ciprofloxacin is contraindicated in persons with a history of hypersensitivity to ciprofloxacin, any member of the quinolone class of antibacterials, or any of the product components [see Warnings and Precautions ( 5.7 )] .

    From the Cipro label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Tizanidine is cleared by CYP1A2. Ciprofloxacin raises its exposure about 10-fold and fluvoxamine about 33-fold. The tizanidine label contraindicates both.

    What to do

    Do not use together.
