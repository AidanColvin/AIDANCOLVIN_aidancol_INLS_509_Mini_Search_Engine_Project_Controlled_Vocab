    Multaq, Calan SR, Lanoxin

    3 heart-rate-slowing drugs: risk of slow heart rate (bradycardia) and heart block

    Why D: The Drug Interactions section of the Multaq label addresses this combination: risk of slow heart rate (bradycardia) and heart block. Clinical rulebook row 20.

    Beta-blockers: Concomitant therapy with beta-adrenergic blockers and verapamil may result in additive negative effects on heart rate, atrioventricular conduction and/or cardiac contractility.

    From the Calan SR label, Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug slows the heart rate or conduction through the AV node. Together they can cause severe bradycardia, AV block, or heart failure.

    What to do

    Check pulse and an ECG after starting or raising any of them; lower doses or stop one if the heart rate drops below 50 or new AV block appears.

    FDA labels: Multaq · Calan SR · Lanoxin

    Zocor with 2 drugs that raise the statin level: raises the statin level: risk of muscle injury

    Why D: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Zocor label addresses this combination: raises the statin level: risk of muscle injury. Clinical rulebook row 27.

    Simvastatin dosage modifications are recommended for patients taking lomitapide, verapamil, diltiazem, dronedarone, amiodarone, amlodipine or ranolazine [see DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION ( 2.5 )].

    From the Zocor label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    These drugs slow simvastatin's clearance. The simvastatin label caps the dose at 10 mg a day with verapamil, diltiazem, or dronedarone, and 20 mg with amiodarone, amlodipine, or ranolazine.

    What to do

    Keep simvastatin at or below the label's cap, or switch to a statin not cleared by CYP3A4 (pravastatin, rosuvastatin).

  3. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Pradaxa, Multaq

    Pradaxa with Multaq: dabigatran levels rise: risk of major bleeding

    Why D: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Pradaxa label addresses this combination: dabigatran levels rise: risk of major bleeding. Clinical rulebook row 11.

    Reduction of Risk of Stroke and Systemic Embolism in Non-valvular Atrial Fibrillation in Adult Patients Reduce the dosage of Dabigatran Etexilate Capsules to 75 mg twice daily when dronedarone or systemic ketoconazole is co-administered with Dabigatran Etexilate Capsules in patients with moderate renal impairment (CrCl 30 to 50 mL/min).

    From the Pradaxa label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Dabigatran is moved out of the gut by P-glycoprotein (P-gp) and cleared about 80% by the kidney. Dronedarone and ketoconazole block P-gp and roughly double dabigatran exposure.

    What to do

    Avoid when kidney function is reduced (CrCl below 50 mL/min); otherwise follow the label's dose reduction and watch for bleeding.

    FDA labels: Pradaxa · Multaq

    Multaq, Lanoxin

    Lanoxin with Multaq: digoxin levels rise: toxicity

    Why D: The Drug Interactions section of the Lanoxin label addresses this combination: digoxin levels rise: toxicity. Clinical rulebook row 18.

    7.2 Pharmacokinetic Drug Interactions Digoxin concentrations increased greater than 50% Digoxin Serum Concentration Increase Digoxin AUC Increase Recommendations Amiodarone 70% NA Measure serum digoxin concentrations before initiating concomitant drugs.

    From the Lanoxin label, Drug Interactions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    These drugs block P-glycoprotein, the pump that clears digoxin, and raise digoxin levels by 50 to 150% (amiodarone about 70%, dronedarone about 2.5-fold). Digoxin has a narrow margin.

    What to do

    Halve the digoxin dose when starting amiodarone or dronedarone, and check a digoxin level within 1 to 2 weeks (target 0.5 to 0.9 ng/mL in heart failure).

    FDA labels: Multaq · Lanoxin · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

    Multaq, Lanoxin, Lasix

    3 drugs: low potassium and magnesium from the diuretic raise the rhythm risk

    Why C: The Precautions section of the Lasix label addresses this combination: low potassium and magnesium from the diuretic raise the rhythm risk. Clinical rulebook row 18, 23.

    Hypokalemia may develop with furosemide, especially with brisk diuresis, inadequate oral electrolyte intake, when cirrhosis is present, or during concomitant use of corticosteroids, ACTH, licorice in large amounts, or prolonged use of laxatives.

    From the Lasix label, Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Loop and thiazide diuretics lower potassium and magnesium. Low levels make QT-prolonging drugs more likely to cause torsades and make digoxin more toxic.

    What to do

    Check potassium and magnesium at the start and after dose changes; replace them to keep potassium above 4.0 mmol/L.

    FDA labels: Multaq · Lanoxin · Lasix
