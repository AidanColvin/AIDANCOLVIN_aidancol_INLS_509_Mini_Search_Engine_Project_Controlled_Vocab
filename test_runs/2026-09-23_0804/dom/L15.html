    Why D: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Toprol-XL label addresses this combination: risk of slow heart rate (bradycardia) and heart block. Clinical rulebook row 20.

    ( 5.3 ) Concomitant use of glycosides, clonidine, and diltiazem and verapamil with beta-blockers can increase the risk of bradycardia.

    From the Toprol-XL label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug slows the heart rate or conduction through the AV node. Together they can cause severe bradycardia, AV block, or heart failure.

    What to do

    Check pulse and an ECG after starting or raising any of them; lower doses or stop one if the heart rate drops below 50 or new AV block appears.

    • Toprol-XL with Cardizem CD: severe bradycardia, heart block, heart failure. Beta-blockers and verapamil or diltiazem both slow the AV node and weaken heart contraction. Together they can cause profound bradycardia, AV block, or cardiogenic shock.

    Lanoxin, Cordarone

    Lanoxin with Cordarone: digoxin levels rise: toxicity

    Why D: The Drug Interactions section of the Lanoxin label addresses this combination: digoxin levels rise: toxicity. Clinical rulebook row 18.

    7.2 Pharmacokinetic Drug Interactions Digoxin concentrations increased greater than 50% Digoxin Serum Concentration Increase Digoxin AUC Increase Recommendations Amiodarone 70% NA Measure serum digoxin concentrations before initiating concomitant drugs.

    From the Lanoxin label, Drug Interactions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    These drugs block P-glycoprotein, the pump that clears digoxin, and raise digoxin levels by 50 to 150% (amiodarone about 70%, dronedarone about 2.5-fold). Digoxin has a narrow margin.

    What to do

    Halve the digoxin dose when starting amiodarone or dronedarone, and check a digoxin level within 1 to 2 weeks (target 0.5 to 0.9 ng/mL in heart failure).

    FDA labels: Lanoxin · Cordarone · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  3. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Lanoxin, Cordarone, Lasix

    3 drugs: low potassium and magnesium from the diuretic raise the rhythm risk

    Why C: The Precautions section of the Lasix label addresses this combination: low potassium and magnesium from the diuretic raise the rhythm risk. Clinical rulebook row 18, 23.

    Hypokalemia may develop with furosemide, especially with brisk diuresis, inadequate oral electrolyte intake, when cirrhosis is present, or during concomitant use of corticosteroids, ACTH, licorice in large amounts, or prolonged use of laxatives.

    From the Lasix label, Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Loop and thiazide diuretics lower potassium and magnesium. Low levels make QT-prolonging drugs more likely to cause torsades and make digoxin more toxic.

    What to do

    Check potassium and magnesium at the start and after dose changes; replace them to keep potassium above 4.0 mmol/L.

    FDA labels: Lanoxin · Cordarone · Lasix

  5. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Toprol-XL, Cordarone

    Toprol-XL with Cordarone: metoprolol levels rise: slower heart rate, lower blood pressure

    Why C: The Drug Interactions section of the Toprol-XL label addresses this combination: metoprolol levels rise: slower heart rate, lower blood pressure.

    ( 7.1 ) CYP2D6 Inhibitors are likely to increase metoprolol concentration.

    From the Toprol-XL label, Drug Interactions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Metoprolol is cleared by CYP2D6; inhibitors raise its level 2- to 5-fold.

    What to do

    Check heart rate and blood pressure; lower the metoprolol dose or switch to a beta-blocker not cleared by CYP2D6 (atenolol, bisoprolol).

    FDA labels: Toprol-XL · Cordarone · FDA: Drug Development and Drug Interactions, Table of Substrates, Inhibitors and Inducers

  6. AMinor — Minor: usually no action needed.

    Lanoxin, Cordarone

    QT Prolongation Risk shared by 2 drugs

    Why A: These labels each mention this risk, but no class rule applies to this combination.

    5.4 Worsened Arrhythmia Amiodarone hydrochloride can exacerbate the presenting arrhythmia in about 2 to 5% of patients or cause new ventricular fibrillation, incessant ventricular tachycardia, increased resistance to cardioversion, and polymorphic ventricular tachycardia associated with QTc prolongation (Torsade de Pointes [TdP]).

    From the Cordarone label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    What this means: QT prolongation

    Each of these drugs can delay the heart's electrical recovery after a beat, seen as a longer QT interval on an ECG. Together the delay can add up and trigger torsades de pointes, a dangerous heart rhythm.

    Source: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, NIH
