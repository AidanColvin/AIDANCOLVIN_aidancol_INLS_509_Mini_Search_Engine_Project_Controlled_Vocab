# Interview Notes: Inpatient Prescription Management and Drug-to-Drug Interactions

- **Date:** 09-21-2026
- **Interviewee:** Attending physician, internal medicine hospitalist
- **Experience:** 25+ years
- **Setting:** Inpatient care in a major hospital system
- **Topic:** How the inpatient setting handles patient prescriptions and drug-to-drug interactions
- **In-text citation (AMA):** (Internal medicine hospitalist, oral communication, September 21, 2026)

## Role and Setting

- The physician works as an internal medicine hospitalist in a major hospital system.
- The physician oversees patient care for the entire duration of the inpatient stay.

## Medication Management

- The physician manages active medication lists and evaluates safety risks.
- Checking drug-to-drug interactions is a daily part of inpatient management.
- Clinical expertise combines with electronic decision support tools to guide treatment.

## Drug Interaction Checking with Lexicomp

- Physicians use Lexicomp within UpToDate.
- The physician enters a patient's prescription drugs and can add more drugs to the list.
- Lexicomp draws on a standard reference for medicine.
- Lexicomp shows which drugs interact and how.
- Lexicomp rates each interaction on a scale of A through D.
