    Levaquin, Deltasone

    Levaquin with Deltasone: tendinitis and tendon rupture

    Why D: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Levaquin label addresses this combination: tendinitis and tendon rupture. Clinical rulebook row 36.

    The risk of developing fluoroquinolone-associated tendinitis and tendon rupture is increased in patients over 60 years of age, in those taking corticosteroid drugs, and in patients with kidney, heart or lung transplants.

    From the Levaquin label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Fluoroquinolones injure tendon tissue; the risk is highest with corticosteroids and after age 60. Fluoroquinolone labels carry a boxed warning.

    What to do

    Choose another antibiotic if possible; tell the patient to stop and call at the first sign of tendon pain.
