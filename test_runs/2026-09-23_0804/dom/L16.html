    Viagra, Nitrostat

    Nitrostat with Viagra: severe, refractory low blood pressure

    Why E: The Contraindications section of the Viagra label addresses this combination: severe, refractory low blood pressure. Clinical rulebook row 21.

    4 CONTRAINDICATIONS Sildenafil for oral suspension is contraindicated in patients with: Concomitant use of organic nitrates in any form, either regularly or intermittently, because of the greater risk of hypotension [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) ] .

    From the Viagra label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Nitrates release nitric oxide, which raises cGMP; PDE5 inhibitors block cGMP breakdown (riociguat stimulates its production). Together blood pressure can fall sharply and not respond to treatment. The labels contraindicate these combinations.

    What to do

    Do not use together. Allow 24 hours after sildenafil or vardenafil and 48 hours after tadalafil before any nitrate.

    FDA labels: Viagra · Nitrostat · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  3. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Catapres, Coreg

    Catapres with Coreg: slow heart rate, and a rebound hypertensive crisis if clonidine stops suddenly

    Why D: The Warnings section of the Catapres label addresses this combination: slow heart rate, and a rebound hypertensive crisis if clonidine stops suddenly. Clinical rulebook row 25.

    The likelihood of such reactions to discontinuation of clonidine therapy appears to be greater after administration of higher doses or continuation of concomitant beta-blocker treatment and special caution is therefore advised in these situations.

    From the Catapres label, Warnings section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Both slow the heart. If clonidine is stopped abruptly, norepinephrine surges; with alpha receptors unopposed under beta-blockade, blood pressure can rebound to a crisis.

    What to do

    Monitor heart rate. To stop, taper the beta-blocker first and then clonidine over several days.

    FDA labels: Catapres · Coreg

    Viagra, Nitrostat, Imdur, Flomax, Catapres, Coreg

    6 drugs: risk of low blood pressure on standing, fainting, and falls

    Why C: The Contraindications section of the Viagra label addresses this combination: risk of low blood pressure on standing, fainting, and falls. Clinical rulebook row 22.

    4 CONTRAINDICATIONS Sildenafil for oral suspension is contraindicated in patients with: Concomitant use of organic nitrates in any form, either regularly or intermittently, because of the greater risk of hypotension [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) ] .

    From the Viagra label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug lowers blood pressure or blocks the reflex that holds it up when standing. Together they can cause orthostatic hypotension, fainting, and falls.

    What to do

    Start low, raise one drug at a time, check standing blood pressure, and tell the patient to stand up slowly.

    • Flomax with Viagra: symptomatic low blood pressure on standing. Alpha-1 blockers and PDE5 inhibitors both widen blood vessels. The sildenafil label advises being on a stable alpha-blocker dose first and starting at the lowest PDE5 dose.

    FDA labels: Viagra · Nitrostat · Imdur · Flomax · Catapres · Coreg

  5. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Catapres, Coreg

    2 heart-rate-slowing drugs: risk of slow heart rate (bradycardia) and heart block

    Why C: The Precautions section of the Catapres label addresses this combination: risk of slow heart rate (bradycardia) and heart block. Clinical rulebook row 20.

    The sympatholytic action of clonidine may worsen sinus node dysfunction and atrioventricular (AV) block, especially in patients taking other sympatholytic drugs.

    From the Catapres label, Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug slows the heart rate or conduction through the AV node. Together they can cause severe bradycardia, AV block, or heart failure.

    What to do

    Check pulse and an ECG after starting or raising any of them; lower doses or stop one if the heart rate drops below 50 or new AV block appears.

    FDA labels: Catapres · Coreg
