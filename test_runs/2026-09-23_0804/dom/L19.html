    Ultram, Seroquel

    2 CNS depressants: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose

    Why D: The Boxed Warning section of the Ultram label addresses this combination: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose. Clinical rulebook row 1, 2.

    ( 5.3 ) Concomitant use of opioids with benzodiazepines or other central nervous system (CNS) depressants, including alcohol, may result in profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death.

    From the Ultram label, Boxed Warning section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug depresses the brain on its own; together the effects add up. Opioids with benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, or other sedatives can stop breathing: both opioid and benzodiazepine labels carry a boxed warning on this combination.

    What to do

    Use the lowest doses for the shortest time, avoid adding more sedatives, and watch for sleepiness and slow or shallow breathing. With an opioid, co-prescribe naloxone.

    FDA labels: Ultram · Seroquel · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  3. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Ultram, Cipro

    Ultram with Cipro: opioid levels rise: risk of fatal respiratory depression

    Why D: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Ultram label addresses this combination: opioid levels rise: risk of fatal respiratory depression. Clinical rulebook row 3.

    Evaluate patients receiving tramadol hydrochloride tablets and any CYP3A4 inhibitor or inducer at frequent intervals for the risk for serious adverse events including seizures and serotonin syndrome, signs and symptoms that may reflect opioid toxicity and opioid withdrawal when tramadol hydrochloride tablets are used in conjunction with inhibitors and inducers of CYP3A4 [see Drug Interactions (7)].

    From the Ultram label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    These opioids are cleared by CYP3A4. A CYP3A4 inhibitor raises opioid levels (fentanyl and oxycodone roughly 2 to 3 times), and the effect can build over days. The opioid labels carry a boxed warning on CYP3A4 inhibitors.

    What to do

    Avoid if possible; otherwise lower the opioid dose, monitor closely for sedation and slow breathing, and re-adjust when the inhibitor stops.

    FDA labels: Ultram · Cipro · FDA: Drug Development and Drug Interactions, Table of Substrates, Inhibitors and Inducers

    Why it happens

    Theophylline is cleared by CYP1A2 and has a narrow margin. Ciprofloxacin raises levels 20 to 100% or more, and fluvoxamine even more; toxicity causes seizures and arrhythmias.

    What to do

    Cipro, Seroquel

    2 QT-prolonging drugs: risk of QT prolongation and torsades de pointes

    Why C: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Cipro label addresses this combination: risk of QT prolongation and torsades de pointes. Clinical rulebook row 23.

    Avoid use in patients with known prolongation, those with hypokalemia, and with other drugs that prolong the QT interval.

    From the Cipro label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug slows the heart's electrical recovery (the QT interval). Together the effect adds up and can trigger torsades de pointes, a dangerous heart rhythm.

    What to do

    Get a baseline ECG and keep potassium and magnesium normal. Stop or switch a drug if the QTc passes 500 ms.

    FDA labels: Cipro · Seroquel · Dechanont et al., Pharmacoepidemiol Drug Saf 2014: drug interactions in hospital admissions

    Ultram with Wellbutrin XL: less pain relief; more parent-drug side effects

    Why C: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Ultram label addresses this combination: less pain relief; more parent-drug side effects. Clinical rulebook row 8, 35.

    Risks of Concomitant Use or Discontinuation of Cytochrome P450 2D6 Inhibitors The concomitant use of tramadol hydrochloride tablets with all cytochrome P450 2D6 inhibitors (e.g., amiodarone, quinidine) may result in an increase in tramadol plasma levels and a decrease in the levels of the active metabolite, M1.

    From the Ultram label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    CYP2D6 turns codeine into morphine and tramadol into its active opioid metabolite. Blocking it reduces pain relief, and for tramadol leaves more parent drug with its seizure and serotonin effects.

    What to do

    Expect weaker analgesia; watch for serotonin effects with tramadol, and consider a different analgesic.

    FDA labels: Wellbutrin XL · Ultram · FDA: Drug Development and Drug Interactions, Table of Substrates, Inhibitors and Inducers
