Why it happens

CYP2D6 turns codeine into morphine and tramadol into its active opioid metabolite. Blocking it reduces pain relief, and for tramadol leaves more parent drug with its seizure and serotonin effects.

What to do

Expect weaker analgesia; watch for serotonin effects with tramadol, and consider a different analgesic.

What this means: Serotonin syndrome

Too much serotonin activity in the nervous system, most often when two or more serotonergic drugs are taken together. Signs include agitation, fast heartbeat, high temperature, sweating, and twitching muscles. It can be life-threatening.
