  1. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Imitrex, Maxalt, Elavil

    3 serotonergic drugs: risk of serotonin syndrome

    Why D: Class rule: risk of serotonin syndrome. Clinical rulebook row 7.

    5.7 Serotonin Syndrome Serotonin syndrome may occur with Sumatriptan Nasal Spray, particularly during coadministration with selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs), serotonin norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors (SNRIs), tricyclic antidepressants (TCAs), and MAO inhibitors [see Drug Interactions (7.4) ] .

    From the Imitrex label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug raises serotonin activity. Together they can cause serotonin syndrome: agitation, tremor, clonus and overactive reflexes, sweating, fever, fast heart rate.

    What to do

    Avoid stacking serotonergic drugs where possible; if needed, use low doses and watch for clonus, tremor, fever, and agitation, especially in the first days after a start or dose increase.

    FDA labels: Imitrex · Maxalt · Elavil · Dunkley et al., QJM 2003: the Hunter serotonin toxicity criteria

  3. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Elavil, Topamax, Fioricet

    3 CNS depressants: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose

    Why C: Class rule: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose. Clinical rulebook row 1, 2.

    Amitriptyline hydrochloride may enhance the response to alcohol and the effects of barbiturates and other CNS depressants.

    From the Elavil label, Warnings section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug depresses the brain on its own; together the effects add up. Opioids with benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, or other sedatives can stop breathing: both opioid and benzodiazepine labels carry a boxed warning on this combination.

    What to do

    Use the lowest doses for the shortest time, avoid adding more sedatives, and watch for sleepiness and slow or shallow breathing. With an opioid, co-prescribe naloxone.

    FDA labels: Elavil · Topamax · Fioricet · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  4. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Inderal LA, Elavil

    2 drugs: risk of low blood pressure on standing, fainting, and falls

    Why C: Class rule: risk of low blood pressure on standing, fainting, and falls. Clinical rulebook row 22.

    Why it happens

    Each drug lowers blood pressure or blocks the reflex that holds it up when standing. Together they can cause orthostatic hypotension, fainting, and falls.

    What to do

    Start low, raise one drug at a time, check standing blood pressure, and tell the patient to stand up slowly.

    FDA labels: Inderal LA · Elavil

  5. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Maxalt, Inderal LA

    Maxalt with Inderal LA: rizatriptan levels rise about 70%

    Why C: The Drug Interactions section of the Maxalt label addresses this combination: rizatriptan levels rise about 70%.

    7 DRUG INTERACTIONS 7.1 Propranolol The dose of rizatriptan benzoate should be adjusted in propranolol-treated patients, as propranolol has been shown to increase the plasma AUC of rizatriptan by 70% [see Dosage and Administration (2.4) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3) ] .

    From the Maxalt label, Drug Interactions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Propranolol inhibits the MAO-A metabolism of rizatriptan and raises its levels about 70%.

    What to do

    Use rizatriptan 5 mg per dose, at most 15 mg a day, as the label directs.

    FDA labels: Maxalt · Inderal LA
