Why it happens

Simvastatin and lovastatin are cleared almost entirely by CYP3A4. Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (and erythromycin, cyclosporine, gemfibrozil, danazol) raise their levels roughly 10-fold, causing muscle breakdown and kidney failure. The simvastatin label contraindicates these combinations.

What to do

Do not use together. Hold the statin during the antibiotic or antifungal course, or switch to pravastatin or rosuvastatin.

From the Aldactone label, Contraindications section.

Why it happens

ACE inhibitors and ARBs lower aldosterone, spironolactone and eplerenone block it, trimethoprim blocks the kidney's potassium channel (like amiloride), and supplements add potassium. Together potassium can rise to levels that stop the heart.

What to do

Check potassium and kidney function within 3 days and again at 1 week; stop the potassium supplement or change the antibiotic when possible.
