    Strattera, Paxil

    Strattera with Paxil: atomoxetine levels rise 6- to 8-fold

    Why D: The Drug Interactions section of the Strattera label addresses this combination: atomoxetine levels rise 6- to 8-fold.

    ( 4 , 7 ) Strong CYP2D6 Inhibitors: With concomitant use of atomoxetine capsules and strong CYP2D6 inhibitors, increase the titration intervals.

    From the Strattera label, Drug Interactions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Atomoxetine is cleared by CYP2D6. Strong inhibitors raise exposure 6- to 8-fold, like a poor metabolizer: faster heart rate, higher blood pressure, side effects.

    What to do

    Start atomoxetine at 40 mg a day and raise to 80 mg only after 4 weeks if needed, as the label directs.

    FDA labels: Strattera · Paxil · FDA: Drug Development and Drug Interactions, Table of Substrates, Inhibitors and Inducers

  3. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Vyvanse, Paxil

    2 serotonergic drugs: risk of serotonin syndrome

    Why C: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Paxil label addresses this combination: risk of serotonin syndrome. Clinical rulebook row 7.

    Serotonin Syndrome: Increased risk when co-administered with serotonergic agents (e.g., SSRIs, SNRIs, triptans), but also during overdosage situations.

    From the Vyvanse label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug raises serotonin activity. Together they can cause serotonin syndrome: agitation, tremor, clonus and overactive reflexes, sweating, fever, fast heart rate.

    What to do

    Avoid stacking serotonergic drugs where possible; if needed, use low doses and watch for clonus, tremor, fever, and agitation, especially in the first days after a start or dose increase.

    FDA labels: Vyvanse · Paxil · Dunkley et al., QJM 2003: the Hunter serotonin toxicity criteria

    Why A: These labels each mention this risk, but no class rule applies to this combination.

    From the Paxil label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed
