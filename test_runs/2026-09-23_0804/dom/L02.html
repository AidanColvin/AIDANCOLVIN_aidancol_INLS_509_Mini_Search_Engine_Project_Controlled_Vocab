  1. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Ultram, Flexeril

    2 CNS depressants: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose

    Why D: The Boxed Warning section of the Ultram label addresses this combination: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose. Clinical rulebook row 1, 2.

    ( 5.3 ) Concomitant use of opioids with benzodiazepines or other central nervous system (CNS) depressants, including alcohol, may result in profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death.

    From the Ultram label, Boxed Warning section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug depresses the brain on its own; together the effects add up. Opioids with benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, or other sedatives can stop breathing: both opioid and benzodiazepine labels carry a boxed warning on this combination.

    What to do

    Use the lowest doses for the shortest time, avoid adding more sedatives, and watch for sleepiness and slow or shallow breathing. With an opioid, co-prescribe naloxone.

    FDA labels: Ultram · Flexeril · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  2. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Zoloft, Ultram, Imitrex, Flexeril, Zofran

    5 serotonergic drugs: risk of serotonin syndrome

    Why D: The Contraindications section of the Zoloft label addresses this combination: risk of serotonin syndrome. Clinical rulebook row 7.

    4 CONTRAINDICATIONS Sertraline hydrochloride tablets are contraindicated in patients: Taking, or within 14 days of stopping, MAOIs, (including the MAOIs linezolid and intravenous methylene blue) because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome [See Warnings and Precautions (5.2) , Drug Interactions (7.1) ] .

    From the Zoloft label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug raises serotonin activity. Together they can cause serotonin syndrome: agitation, tremor, clonus and overactive reflexes, sweating, fever, fast heart rate.

    What to do

    Avoid stacking serotonergic drugs where possible; if needed, use low doses and watch for clonus, tremor, fever, and agitation, especially in the first days after a start or dose increase.

    FDA labels: Zoloft · Ultram · Imitrex · Flexeril · Zofran · Dunkley et al., QJM 2003: the Hunter serotonin toxicity criteria

  3. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Zoloft, Ultram

    2 drugs: risk of seizures

    Why C: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Ultram label addresses this combination: risk of seizures. Clinical rulebook row 8.

    Concomitant use with other drugs may increase seizure risk.

    From the Ultram label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Tramadol and bupropion lower the seizure threshold in a dose-related way. The other drugs listed lower it too, so the risk adds up.

    What to do

    Keep tramadol and bupropion at or below labeled doses, avoid them in patients with seizure risk factors, and prefer alternatives when several threshold-lowering drugs are needed.

    FDA labels: Zoloft · Ultram

  4. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Zoloft, Ultram

    Ultram with Zoloft: less pain relief; more parent-drug side effects

    Why C: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Ultram label addresses this combination: less pain relief; more parent-drug side effects. Clinical rulebook row 8, 35.

    Risks of Concomitant Use or Discontinuation of Cytochrome P450 2D6 Inhibitors The concomitant use of tramadol hydrochloride tablets with all cytochrome P450 2D6 inhibitors (e.g., amiodarone, quinidine) may result in an increase in tramadol plasma levels and a decrease in the levels of the active metabolite, M1.

    From the Ultram label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    CYP2D6 turns codeine into morphine and tramadol into its active opioid metabolite. Blocking it reduces pain relief, and for tramadol leaves more parent drug with its seizure and serotonin effects.

    What to do

    Expect weaker analgesia; watch for serotonin effects with tramadol, and consider a different analgesic.

    FDA labels: Zoloft · Ultram · FDA: Drug Development and Drug Interactions, Table of Substrates, Inhibitors and Inducers

  7. AMinor — Minor: usually no action needed.

    Zoloft, Zofran

    QT Prolongation Risk shared by 2 drugs

    Why A: These labels each mention this risk, but no class rule applies to this combination.

    QTc Prolongation: Sertraline hydrochloride should be used with caution in patients with risk factors for QTc prolongation.

    From the Zoloft label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    What this means: QT prolongation

    Each of these drugs can delay the heart's electrical recovery after a beat, seen as a longer QT interval on an ECG. Together the delay can add up and trigger torsades de pointes, a dangerous heart rhythm.

    Source: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, NIH

    FDA labels: Zoloft · Zofran
