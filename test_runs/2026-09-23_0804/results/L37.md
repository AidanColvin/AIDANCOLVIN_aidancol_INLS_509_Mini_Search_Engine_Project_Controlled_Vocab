Reduction of Risk of Stroke and Systemic Embolism in Non-valvular Atrial Fibrillation in Adult Patients Reduce the dosage of Dabigatran Etexilate Capsules to 75 mg twice daily when dronedarone or systemic ketoconazole is co-administered with Dabigatran Etexilate Capsules in patients with moderate renal impairment (CrCl 30 to 50 mL/min).

From the Pradaxa label, Warnings and Precautions section.

Why it happens

Dabigatran is moved out of the gut by P-glycoprotein (P-gp) and cleared about 80% by the kidney. Dronedarone and ketoconazole block P-gp and roughly double dabigatran exposure.

What to do

Avoid when kidney function is reduced (CrCl below 50 mL/min); otherwise follow the label's dose reduction and watch for bleeding.

Why D: The Drug Interactions section of the Lanoxin label addresses this combination: digoxin levels rise: toxicity. Clinical rulebook row 18.

7.2 Pharmacokinetic Drug Interactions Digoxin concentrations increased greater than 50% Digoxin Serum Concentration Increase Digoxin AUC Increase Recommendations Amiodarone 70% NA Measure serum digoxin concentrations before initiating concomitant drugs.

From the Lanoxin label, Drug Interactions section.
Open on DailyMed

Why it happens

These drugs block P-glycoprotein, the pump that clears digoxin, and raise digoxin levels by 50 to 150% (amiodarone about 70%, dronedarone about 2.5-fold). Digoxin has a narrow margin.

What to do

Halve the digoxin dose when starting amiodarone or dronedarone, and check a digoxin level within 1 to 2 weeks (target 0.5 to 0.9 ng/mL in heart failure).
