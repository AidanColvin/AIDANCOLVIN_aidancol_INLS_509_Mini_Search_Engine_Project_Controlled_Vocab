  1. EAvoid combination — Do not use these drugs together.

    Nardil, Demerol

    Nardil with Demerol: can cause fatal serotonin syndrome

    Why E: The Contraindications section of the Demerol label addresses this combination: can cause fatal serotonin syndrome. Clinical rulebook row 7.

    ( 4 ) • Concurrent use of monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs) or use of MAOIs within the last 14 days.

    From the Demerol label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    An MAO inhibitor stops the breakdown of serotonin; these drugs release it or block its reuptake. Together serotonin can rise to toxic levels: high fever, rigidity, seizures, death.

    What to do

    Do not use together. Allow the washout the labels require (usually 14 days after stopping an MAO inhibitor, 5 weeks after fluoxetine).

    FDA labels: Nardil · Demerol · Dunkley et al., QJM 2003: the Hunter serotonin toxicity criteria · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  2. EAvoid combination — Do not use these drugs together.

    Nardil, Wellbutrin SR

    Nardil with Wellbutrin SR: risk of a hypertensive crisis

    Why E: The Contraindications section of the Wellbutrin SR label addresses this combination: risk of a hypertensive crisis. Clinical rulebook row 24.

    There is an increased risk of hypertensive reactions when bupropion hydrobromide extended-release tablets are used concomitantly with MAOIs.

    From the Wellbutrin SR label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Bupropion raises norepinephrine and dopamine; with an MAO inhibitor these build up and can cause a sudden, severe rise in blood pressure. The bupropion label contraindicates the combination and requires 14 days between them.

    What to do

    Do not use together. Wait at least 14 days after stopping the MAO inhibitor.

    FDA labels: Nardil · Wellbutrin SR

  3. EAvoid combination — Do not use these drugs together.

    Nardil, Ritalin

    Nardil with Ritalin: risk of a hypertensive crisis

    Why E: The Contraindications section of the Ritalin label addresses this combination: risk of a hypertensive crisis. Clinical rulebook row 24.

    Concomitant treatment with monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs), and also within a minimum of 14 days following discontinuation of treatment with a monoamine oxidase inhibitor because of the risk of hypertensive crisis [see Drug Interactions (7.1) ].

    From the Ritalin label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    MAO inhibitors stop the breakdown of norepinephrine; stimulants and decongestants release it. Together blood pressure can spike to a hypertensive crisis with stroke or heart damage.

    What to do

    Do not use together, or within 14 days of stopping the MAO inhibitor.

    FDA labels: Nardil · Ritalin

  4. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Nardil, Demerol

    2 serotonergic drugs: risk of serotonin syndrome

    Why D: Class rule: risk of serotonin syndrome. Clinical rulebook row 7.

    Why it happens

    Each drug raises serotonin activity. Together they can cause serotonin syndrome: agitation, tremor, clonus and overactive reflexes, sweating, fever, fast heart rate.

    What to do

    Avoid stacking serotonergic drugs where possible; if needed, use low doses and watch for clonus, tremor, fever, and agitation, especially in the first days after a start or dose increase.

    FDA labels: Nardil · Demerol · Dunkley et al., QJM 2003: the Hunter serotonin toxicity criteria

  5. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Nardil, Norvasc

    2 drugs: risk of low blood pressure on standing, fainting, and falls

    Why C: The Contraindications section of the Nardil label addresses this combination: risk of low blood pressure on standing, fainting, and falls. Clinical rulebook row 22.

    A List of MAO Inhibitors by Generic Name Follows: pargyline hydrochloride pargyline hydrochloride and methylclothiazide furazolidone isocarboxazid procarbazine tranylcypromine Phenelzine sulfate should also not be used in combination with buspirone HCl, since several cases of elevated blood pressure have been reported in patients taking MAO inhibitors who were then given buspirone HCl.

    From the Nardil label, Contraindications section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug lowers blood pressure or blocks the reflex that holds it up when standing. Together they can cause orthostatic hypotension, fainting, and falls.

    What to do

    Start low, raise one drug at a time, check standing blood pressure, and tell the patient to stand up slowly.

    FDA labels: Nardil · Norvasc

  6. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Wellbutrin SR, Demerol

    2 drugs: risk of seizures

    Why C: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Wellbutrin SR label addresses this combination: risk of seizures. Clinical rulebook row 8.

    The risk of seizures is also related to patient factors, clinical situations, and concomitant medications that lower the seizure threshold.

    From the Wellbutrin SR label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Tramadol and bupropion lower the seizure threshold in a dose-related way. The other drugs listed lower it too, so the risk adds up.

    What to do

    Keep tramadol and bupropion at or below labeled doses, avoid them in patients with seizure risk factors, and prefer alternatives when several threshold-lowering drugs are needed.

    FDA labels: Wellbutrin SR · Demerol
