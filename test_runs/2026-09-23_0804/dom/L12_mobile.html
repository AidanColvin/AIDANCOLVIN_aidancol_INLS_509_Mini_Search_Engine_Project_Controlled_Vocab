    Symbyax, Norco, Xanax, Xanax XR

    3 CNS depressants: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose

    Why D: The Boxed Warning section of the Norco label addresses this combination: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose. Clinical rulebook row 1, 2.

    Risks from Concomitant Use with Benzodiazepines or Other CNS Depressants Concomitant use of opioids with benzodiazepines or other central nervous system (CNS) depressants, including alcohol, may result in profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death.

    From the Norco label, Boxed Warning section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug depresses the brain on its own; together the effects add up. Opioids with benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, or other sedatives can stop breathing: both opioid and benzodiazepine labels carry a boxed warning on this combination.

    What to do

    Use the lowest doses for the shortest time, avoid adding more sedatives, and watch for sleepiness and slow or shallow breathing. With an opioid, co-prescribe naloxone.

    • Norco with 2 benzodiazepines: boxed warning: profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, death. Opioids and benzodiazepines both suppress the brainstem's drive to breathe. Both drug classes carry an FDA boxed warning against this combination.

    FDA labels: Symbyax · Norco · Xanax · Xanax XR · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  6. BMonitor — Real but manageable: monitor.

    Symbyax, Prozac, Norco

    Norco with 2 drugs that block CYP2D6: may change the opioid's effect

    Why B: The Precautions section of the Norco label addresses this combination: may change the opioid's effect. Clinical rulebook row 35.

    These effects could be more pronounced with concomitant use of hydrocodone bitartrate and acetaminophen oral solution and both CYP3A4 and CYP2D6 inhibitors, particularly when an inhibitor is added after a stable dose of hydrocodone bitartrate and acetaminophen oral solution is achieved [see WARNINGS ].

    From the Norco label, Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    CYP2D6 makes a minor active metabolite (hydromorphone from hydrocodone). Blocking it can reduce analgesia somewhat.

    What to do

    Monitor pain control.

    Why A: These labels each mention this risk, but no class rule applies to this combination.

    Monitor weight regularly ( 5.5 ) Serotonin Syndrome : Serotonin syndrome has been reported with SSRIs and SNRIs, including olanzapine and fluoxetine capsules, both when taken alone, but especially when co-administered with other serotonergic agents.

    From the Symbyax label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    What this means: Serotonin syndrome

    Too much serotonin activity in the nervous system, most often when two or more serotonergic drugs are taken together. Signs include agitation, fast heartbeat, high temperature, sweating, and twitching muscles. It can be life-threatening.

    Source: MedlinePlus, National Library of Medicine
