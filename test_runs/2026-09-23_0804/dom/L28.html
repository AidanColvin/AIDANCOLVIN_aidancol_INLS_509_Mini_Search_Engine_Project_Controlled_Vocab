    Prezcobix, Lipitor

    Lipitor with Prezcobix: atorvastatin levels rise: risk of muscle injury

    Why D: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Lipitor label addresses this combination: atorvastatin levels rise: risk of muscle injury. Clinical rulebook row 27.

    Concomitant use of cyclosporine, gemfibrozil, tipranavir plus ritonavir, or glecaprevir plus pibrentasvir with atorvastatin calcium tablets is not recommended.

    From the Lipitor label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors raise atorvastatin exposure several-fold. The atorvastatin label limits the dose (20 mg with clarithromycin, itraconazole, or boosted protease inhibitors) and advises avoiding cyclosporine, gemfibrozil, and tipranavir.

    What to do

    Use the lowest atorvastatin dose (at most 20 mg with these), or switch to pravastatin or rosuvastatin; watch for muscle pain and dark urine.

    Prezcobix, Flonase

    Flonase with Prezcobix: steroid builds up: Cushing's syndrome and adrenal suppression

    Why D: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Flonase label addresses this combination: steroid builds up: Cushing's syndrome and adrenal suppression.

    5.6 Drug Interactions with Strong Cytochrome P450 3A4 Inhibitors The use of strong cytochrome P450 3A4 (CYP3A4) inhibitors (e.g., ritonavir, atazanavir, clarithromycin, indinavir, itraconazole, nefazodone, nelfinavir, saquinavir, ketoconazole, telithromycin, conivaptan, lopinavir, nefazodone, voriconazole) with fluticasone propionate nasal spray is not recommended because increased systemic corticosteroid adverse effects may occur [see Drug Interactions ( 7.1 ), Clinical Pharmacology ( 12.3 )] .

    From the Flonase label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Ritonavir and cobicistat block CYP3A4 clearance of fluticasone and budesonide, so even nasal or inhaled doses reach systemic levels and cause Cushing's syndrome and adrenal suppression. The fluticasone label advises against the combination.

    What to do

    Switch to beclomethasone, or stop the nasal or inhaled steroid.

    FDA labels: Prezcobix · Flonase

    Prezcobix, Truvada

    Truvada with Prezcobix: tenofovir exposure rises; creatinine rises

    Why C: The Drug Interactions section of the Truvada label addresses this combination: tenofovir exposure rises; creatinine rises.

    When coadministered with emtricitabine and tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablets, use atazanavir given with ritonavir.

    From the Truvada label, Drug Interactions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Boosters raise tenofovir disoproxil exposure, adding kidney risk, and cobicistat blocks tubular creatinine secretion (MATE1), raising creatinine without a true loss of filtration.

    What to do

    Monitor creatinine, urine protein, and phosphate; consider tenofovir alafenamide.

    FDA labels: Prezcobix · Truvada

    WARNING: RISKS FROM CONCOMITANT USE WITH OPIOIDS; ABUSE, MISUSE, AND ADDICTION; and DEPENDENCE AND WITHDRAWAL REACTIONS Concomitant use of benzodiazepines and opioids may result in profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death.
