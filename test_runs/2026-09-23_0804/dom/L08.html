    Clozaril, Tegretol, Ativan, Depakote

    4 CNS depressants: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose

    Why D: Class rule: risk of oversedation, slowed breathing (respiratory depression), falls, and overdose. Clinical rulebook row 1, 2.

    WARNING: RISKS FROM CONCOMITANT USE WITH OPIOIDS; ABUSE, MISUSE, AND ADDICTION; and DEPENDENCE AND WITHDRAWAL REACTIONS Concomitant use of benzodiazepines and opioids may result in profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death.

    From the Ativan label, Boxed Warning section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug depresses the brain on its own; together the effects add up. Opioids with benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, or other sedatives can stop breathing: both opioid and benzodiazepine labels carry a boxed warning on this combination.

    What to do

    Use the lowest doses for the shortest time, avoid adding more sedatives, and watch for sleepiness and slow or shallow breathing. With an opioid, co-prescribe naloxone.

    • Clozaril with Ativan: collapse, respiratory arrest, and cardiac arrest have been reported. The clozapine label warns of collapse, respiratory arrest, and cardiac arrest with benzodiazepines and other psychotropic drugs, especially early in treatment.

    FDA labels: Clozaril · Tegretol · Ativan · Depakote · Phansalkar et al., JAMIA 2012: high-priority drug-drug interactions (ONC expert panel)

  2. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Clozaril, Tegretol

    Clozaril with Tegretol: agranulocytosis risk adds up, and clozapine levels fall

    Why D: The Drug Interactions section of the Clozaril label addresses this combination: agranulocytosis risk adds up, and clozapine levels fall.

    Discontinuation of CYP1A2 or CYP3A4 Inducers : Consider reducing VERSACLOZ dose when CYP1A2 (e.g., tobacco smoke) or CYP3A4 inducers (e.g., carbamazepine) are discontinued ( 2.8 , 7.1 ).

    From the Clozaril label, Drug Interactions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Both drugs can cause agranulocytosis, and carbamazepine induces CYP3A4 and roughly halves clozapine levels.

    What to do

    Avoid; choose another mood stabilizer.

    FDA labels: Clozaril · Tegretol

  3. DConsider changing therapy — Dangerous: avoid, or use only with a specific reason and close monitoring.

    Clozaril, Luvox, Cipro

    Clozaril with 2 strong CYP1A2 inhibitors: clozapine levels rise 2- to 5-fold: seizures, sedation, low blood pressure

    Why D: The Drug Interactions section of the Clozaril label addresses this combination: clozapine levels rise 2- to 5-fold: seizures, sedation, low blood pressure. Clinical rulebook row 5.

    7 DRUG INTERACTIONS Concomitant use of Strong CYP1A2 Inhibitors : Reduce VERSACLOZ dose to one third when coadministered with strong CYP1A2 inhibitors (e.g., fluvoxamine, ciprofloxacin, enoxacin) ( 2.8 , 7.1 ).

    From the Clozaril label, Drug Interactions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Clozapine is cleared mainly by CYP1A2. Fluvoxamine raises clozapine levels 3- to 5-fold and ciprofloxacin about 2-fold. The label advises a one-third dose with strong CYP1A2 inhibitors.

    What to do

    Reduce clozapine to one-third of the dose, check a clozapine level, and watch for sedation, seizures, and ileus.

    FDA labels: Clozaril · Luvox · Cipro · FDA: Drug Development and Drug Interactions, Table of Substrates, Inhibitors and Inducers

  4. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Clozaril, Cipro

    2 QT-prolonging drugs: risk of QT prolongation and torsades de pointes

    Why C: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Clozaril label addresses this combination: risk of QT prolongation and torsades de pointes. Clinical rulebook row 23.

    Conditions that increase these risks include the following: history of QT prolongation, long QT syndrome, family history of long QT syndrome or sudden cardiac death, significant cardiac arrhythmia, recent myocardial infarction, uncompensated heart failure, treatment with other medications that cause QT prolongation, treatment with medications that inhibit the metabolism of VERSACLOZ, and electrolyte abnormalities.

    From the Clozaril label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    Each drug slows the heart's electrical recovery (the QT interval). Together the effect adds up and can trigger torsades de pointes, a dangerous heart rhythm.

    What to do

    Get a baseline ECG and keep potassium and magnesium normal. Stop or switch a drug if the QTc passes 500 ms.

    FDA labels: Clozaril · Cipro · Dechanont et al., Pharmacoepidemiol Drug Saf 2014: drug interactions in hospital admissions

  5. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Tegretol, Luvox

    2 drugs that lower sodium: risk of low sodium (hyponatremia)

    Why C: Class rule: risk of low sodium (hyponatremia). Clinical rulebook row 17.

    Why it happens

    Thiazides make the kidney lose sodium; SSRIs, SNRIs, carbamazepine, and oxcarbazepine cause the body to hold water (an SIADH-like effect). Together sodium can fall enough to cause confusion, falls, or seizures, most often in older women.

    What to do

    Check sodium at baseline and within 2 weeks; choose a non-thiazide diuretic if sodium falls.

    FDA labels: Tegretol · Luvox

  6. CMonitor closely — Clinically significant: adjust the dose or timing, or monitor a named measure.

    Tegretol, Depakote

    Depakote with Tegretol: valproate levels fall; carbamazepine epoxide rises

    Why C: The Warnings and Precautions section of the Depakote label addresses this combination: valproate levels fall; carbamazepine epoxide rises. Clinical rulebook row 33.

    The largest of these studies 1 is a prospective cohort study conducted in the United States and United Kingdom that found that children with prenatal exposure to valproate (n=62) had lower IQ scores at age 6 (97 [95% CI 94 to 101]) than children with prenatal exposure to the other antiepileptic drug monotherapy treatments evaluated: lamotrigine (108 [95% CI 105 to 110]), carbamazepine (105 [95% CI 102 to 108]), and phenytoin (108 [95% CI 104 to 112]).

    From the Depakote label, Warnings and Precautions section.Open on DailyMed

    Why it happens

    These drugs induce valproate metabolism and lower its levels, and valproate raises carbamazepine's toxic 10,11-epoxide metabolite.

    What to do

    Check drug levels when either is started or changed, and watch for dizziness, double vision, and unsteadiness.

    FDA labels: Tegretol · Depakote
